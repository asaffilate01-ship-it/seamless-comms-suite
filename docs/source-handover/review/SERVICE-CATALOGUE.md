# Service catalogue and claim boundaries

The public website describes services that can be scoped as a focused engagement, a standalone tool or an add-on. That commercial design is distinct from whether every module has been integrated, deployed and activated. Fees and subscription checkout were not invented for this handover.

**Omniqora One** is the umbrella workspace/package combining selected services. A standalone service should share the governed service implementation with its Omniqora add-on, with a separate presentation and billing entitlement where appropriate.

## Business360 intake requirements to preserve

Business identity/purpose and departments precede people/stakeholders. Then establish products/services, customers, channels, workflows, assets (including intangible assets), technology, people/roles, suppliers, contracts, compliance costs, financial inputs, liabilities, debtors/creditors, bottlenecks and short/medium/long-term objectives. Record evidence status and missing facts. Use observed baselines and explicit assumptions for financial/KPI calculations.

Recommendations need action, accountable owner, dependency, estimate/cost, expected benefit, timeframe, evidence and verification criteria. Separate recurring savings, one-off cash release, revenue opportunity and service-quality benefits to avoid double counting. Acquisitions, mergers and carve-outs require an explicit perimeter and reviewed dependencies; the system can generate a structured first plan on day one of an engagement, but cannot establish a complete accurate plan without adequate evidence.

## Access and commercial model

Preserve business/department/person records separately from accounts and access grants. Enforce tenant/project membership and sensitive personnel restrictions server-side and at the database. Do not imply that every person entered can log in or that project roles automatically implement department/document/deal-room permissions. The standalone pilot does not yet provide self-serve billing, SSO/MFA, email invitations or password recovery. These remain product work, not public guarantees.

Sell standalone services, add-ons to our SaaS/apps, and managed delivery with an agreed scope. Keep infrastructure/provider usage and professional services cost visible in commercial design, but do not publish unsupported prices or guaranteed savings.
