# Brand and website review

## Recommended umbrella

**Omniqora**

**Your business, working as one.**

This was already part of the recovered Omniqora One brand direction. It fits communications, business intelligence, operational improvement, governance and complex change. Use sentence-case Omniqora consistently in customer-facing copy; preserve established product names in source identifiers where renaming would break compatibility.

The earlier 'Every conversation. One intelligent core.' remains a useful campaign line for Connect/Voice, but its emphasis on conversations understates the expanded umbrella. Business360 can use the supporting line 'Understand the business. Find the losses. Improve the results.' without becoming a competing parent slogan.

## Logo judgement

Keep the existing abstract connected Q mark. Its connected forms and blue/purple/teal palette can represent people, information and operations as well as communication. The website reuses the original vector `omniqora-mark.svg` from the Full Suite unchanged, accompanied by a clean text wordmark. No new raster logo was generated and no claim of a legally cleared mark is made.

Use the main logo without a tiny embedded slogan in navigation. Put the slogan in the hero, footer, presentations and larger brand lockups. Retain One, Connect, Voice, Flow, Agents and Exchange as the established family. Present Business360, transformation, governance and other specialist services as clear descriptive offerings; avoid creating a separate competing logo for every feature. Omniqora Contact can sit within Voice/contact-centre services, with its exact packaging settled alongside implementation.

The recovered PNG options are preserved for review. Prefer the original vector mark for responsive web use; generated PNG variants may differ in lettering, slogan or background treatment and should not be mixed indiscriminately.

The current source uses an outcome-led homepage, clear service groups, FAQs, public/private area separation, bilingual navigation and an iTechLounge identity. ## Public copy boundary

Publish the business problem, intended result, service scope, examples, engagement options and how to contact the team. Keep implementation details, provider routing, prompts, internal control-plane design, security configuration, private product portfolio and customer data out of the public site. Do not promise that all integrations, payments, telephony, autonomous actions or professional certifications are already available.

The website therefore presents scoped services and says availability/integrations are confirmed for each engagement. Provider activation and actual functionality are documented privately in the service catalogue. There are no invented testimonials, customers, savings percentages or case-study results.

## Observations included

1. A missed enquiry is an operational signal — follow-up and handoffs matter as well as the initial contact.
2. Revenue and cash tell different stories — connect sales, delivery, invoicing and collection.
3. Day 1 starts well before Day 1 — shared systems, contracts, people and data need examination.
4. Start with one useful outcome — a clear owner, problem and measurable result guide AI adoption.

These are original editorial observations, not claimed research findings or customer evidence. They are editable in the website content array; no CMS/editor login is included in this marketing patch.
