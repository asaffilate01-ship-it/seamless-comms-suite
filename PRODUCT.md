# Omniqora — product context

**Users:** Business operators, staff, agents, administrators, and customers using connected communications and operations.

**Primary service:** Connect conversations, approvals, CRM, WhatsApp workflows, AI agents, analytics, and vertical services under governed access.

**User trust:** Information hierarchy for agent actions, approval queues, human handoff, communication consent, inbox status, workflow history, customer privacy, and AI provenance. Never make an unexecuted agent action appear complete.

**Experience to inspect first:** Review unified public homepage, Business360 operations, conversation inbox, workflow canvas, AI-agent activity and connector/setup states; make setup failures and unsupported integrations explicit.

Existing code, actual policy rules and verified integrations are the source of truth, not promotional illustrations. Never silently open protected dashboards or change live billing, consent or database contracts during UI work.
