# Omniqora product structure

## Omniqora Control

The internal iTechLounge control plane. It manages applications, tenants, workflows, AI providers, policy packs, approvals, costs, reliability and audit evidence across the full portfolio. Clients do not receive access to this layer.

## Omniqora Intelligence

The sellable add-on. It can appear inside an iTechLounge SaaS, run as a direct business portal, or use a client's branding. Every customer receives an isolated tenant, industry entitlements, its own usage meter and a subscription separate from the underlying SaaS.

## Distribution models

| Model | Customer experience | Commercial approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Embedded | Intelligence tab inside Dishbee, Haccora, Cirqiva or another SaaS | Monthly add-on plus included usage |
| Direct | Omniqora workspace for a business not using another iTechLounge product | Standalone monthly industry plan |
| Portfolio | One group controls multiple sites, projects or businesses | Platform fee plus per-tenant pricing |
| White label | Client logo, colours, support identity and optional domain | Setup fee, higher subscription and usage |

## Suggested initial packs

- Hospitality Intelligence: sales, demand, stock, labour, menu margin and waste.
- Food Safety Intelligence: failed checks, corrective actions, training and inspection readiness.
- Waste Intelligence: collection exceptions, duty-of-care evidence, diversion and cost reduction.
- Construction Intelligence: drawing revisions, change impact, approvals, programme and risk.
- Property and Grants Intelligence: eligibility, evidence completeness, works and claim tracking.
- Payment Intelligence: recommendation and exception triage only until regulated controls permit more.

Pricing values remain configurable rather than hard-coded. Each plan can include a base allowance, overage rate, feature entitlements and optional white-label capability.
