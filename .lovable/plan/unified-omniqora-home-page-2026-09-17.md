# Unified OmniQora Home Page

## Goal
Merge the promo and services pages into one polished public homepage with clearer navigation, a stronger brand story, and concise capability-led content.

## Build
- Replace the separate “all services” detour with a single coherent page journey.
- Use the selected cyber-corporate asymmetric direction with the locked OmniQora navy, electric indigo and cyan palette.
- Keep Space Grotesk headings and switch body copy to DM Sans.
- Rework navigation around Platform, Services, Solutions, Pricing, FAQ and Contact, with an accessible mobile menu and bottom navigation.
- Introduce a 60/40 first view with the original OmniQora logo, concise positioning, clear actions and a polished product preview.
- Curate platform, AI, Business360, transformation, governance, procurement and revenue capabilities into high-level groups.
- Retain translated pricing, FAQ, access, legal links, language switching and cookie controls.
- Remove public copy that reveals intake stages, implementation mechanics, handoff logic or full workflow/process details.
- Redirect the old services page to the matching section on the unified homepage.

## Quality checks
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, navigation anchors, language switching and access form.
- Check for overflow, overlapping text, console errors and broken links.
- Confirm the home page metadata remains unique and accurate.

## Technical details
- Consolidate presentation in the existing home route and shared promo chrome.
- Extend existing multilingual content only where merged service labels require it.
- Use semantic global tokens and existing design-system controls; no backend changes.
